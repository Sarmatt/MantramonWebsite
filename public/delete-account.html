Delete your Mantramon account

App: Mantramon AR by Mantramon-Company

Option 1 - Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mantramon AR and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Google or Apple you may be asked to re-authenticate once for security.

Option 2 - Request deletion from this page

If you cannot access the app (for example you uninstalled it), send us a request. We will verify ownership of the account and delete it, normally within 30 days.

This opens your email app with a pre-filled message to playmantramon@gmail.com. Please send it from the email address of the account.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email, sign-in provider link, user ID).
  • Your game profile and progress stored in Firestore (nickname, avatar, Mantramons, items, achievements, streaks, statistics, chat history).

What may be retained

  • Purchase records held by Google Play / Apple and legally required transaction records, kept as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics and crash logs that cannot identify you.
  • Backups, which are purged automatically within about 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel active subscriptions. Cancel them in Google Play or the App Store first. See also our Privacy Policy.
