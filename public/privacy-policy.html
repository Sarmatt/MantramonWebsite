Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mantramon-Company ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information in the Mantramon AR mobile application (Android package com.Mantramon.MantramonAR, iOS bundle com.mantramon.mantramonAR) (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

DataDetails
Account dataUnique user ID, email address (if you sign in with email, Google or Apple), sign-in provider, and a password if you register with email (handled only by Firebase Authentication; we never see it). Guests may play with an anonymous ID.
Profile & game dataNickname, avatar, progress, collected Mantramons, achievements, in-game purchases, streaks, settings, leaderboard and battle statistics, and in-app chat messages with game characters.
CameraUsed live on your device to display augmented reality content. Camera images are not recorded or uploaded by us.
LocationUsed for location-based AR features (Geo Anchors) to place AR content. It is not used to profile you.
Device & usage dataDevice model, OS version, app version, advertising ID, IP address, crash reports and diagnostics, and in-app events (via Firebase Analytics, Unity Analytics and Backtrace).
PurchasesPurchase receipts/tokens processed by Google Play or Apple App Store and Unity IAP. We do not receive your payment card details.
Multiplayer dataNickname, avatar and in-match actions are shared with other players in real time through Photon.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve the App, including saving your progress.
  • To enable AR, multiplayer battles, leaderboards, achievements and chat features.
  • To process and restore purchases.
  • To show advertisements and measure their performance.
  • To analyse usage, diagnose crashes, keep the App secure and prevent abuse.
  • To send notifications you have allowed, and to respond to support requests.

3. Third-party services

The App uses third-party services which may collect data under their own policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Remote Config) - firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In, Google Play Games and Google Play services - policies.google.com/privacy
  • Sign in with Apple - apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Unity (Analytics, In-App Purchasing, Cloud Content Delivery, Push Notifications) - unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks, for advertising - Unity privacy policy
  • Photon Engine (multiplayer) - photonengine.com/Legal
  • Backtrace (crash reporting) - backtrace.io/privacy-policy
  • AI chat provider - chat messages you send to in-app characters may be processed by a third-party AI service to generate replies.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads. Ad partners may use your advertising ID to show personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. On iOS we ask for your permission through the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers listed above as needed to run the App, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users. Your nickname, avatar and game statistics may be visible to other players.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time, in the App or on our account deletion page, which explains what is deleted and what is retained.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase security controls. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, email playmantramon@gmail.com. You can revoke camera, location and notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union, with appropriate safeguards where required.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version; material changes will be communicated in the App or on this page.
